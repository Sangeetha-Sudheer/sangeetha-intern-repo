OHS Laptop Ergonomics Reflection

Current desk risks

I currently use a MacBook Air for most of my work. Since I mainly use the laptop screen and keyboard, I sometimes look down for long periods, which can cause neck strain. Long periods of sitting can also lead to shoulder and wrist discomfort.

Equipment that would improve my workspace

Equipment that would improve my setup includes:

- Laptop stand to raise the screen to eye level
- External keyboard
- External mouse
- Ergonomic chair with proper back support

Workspace changes I made

I adjusted my chair so my feet rest flat on the floor and positioned my laptop directly in front of me. I also made sure my workspace was free from clutter.

Habit changes

I now:

- Take regular movement breaks every hour.
- Stretch my neck and shoulders.
- Follow the 20-20-20 rule to reduce eye strain.
- Maintain a more upright sitting posture.

How I remind myself to take breaks

I use reminders to stand up and stretch throughout the day. These reminders help reduce prolonged sitting and improve my posture.

Reflection

Learning about desk ergonomics has helped me understand how small adjustments can prevent discomfort and reduce the risk of long-term injuries. I plan to continue using better posture and regular movement breaks while working.
