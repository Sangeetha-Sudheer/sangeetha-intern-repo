# User Manual for Sangeetha Sudheer

## My Role

I am working as a Backend Developer Intern at Focus Bear.

My work mainly involves backend development, testing, debugging, Git and GitHub workflows, NestJS, APIs, databases, and completing technical onboarding tasks.

I work best when I understand the goal of a task clearly and can work through it step by step.

## How I Work Best

I do my best work when:

- the task requirements are clear and specific;
- I know what the expected result should look like;
- larger tasks are broken into smaller steps;
- I have enough uninterrupted time to focus;
- I can test my work before considering it complete;
- I can ask questions when I am unsure about a requirement.

I sometimes struggle when instructions are very broad or when several unrelated tasks need attention at the same time.

Clear priorities help me stay focused and complete work accurately.

## Communication Style

I prefer communication that is clear, direct, and friendly.

For normal updates, I am comfortable with asynchronous messages such as Teams, GitHub comments, or other written messages.

For complicated technical issues, a short call or screen-sharing session can sometimes be more useful than a long written discussion.

If something is urgent, it helps if the message clearly states what needs attention and the expected deadline.

## Collaboration & Teamwork

I work best in a team when responsibilities are clearly divided and everyone communicates their progress.

I appreciate teammates who:

- communicate when they are blocked;
- share important changes with the group;
- ask questions instead of making assumptions;
- give others enough time to complete their assigned work;
- keep shared documents and task boards updated.

One thing I find difficult in teamwork is when expectations or responsibilities are unclear.

If I seem quiet in a meeting, it usually means I am listening, processing the information, or thinking about the best way to respond.

## Focus & Productivity

I stay focused by:

- working from a clear task list;
- breaking large tasks into smaller steps;
- completing one important task at a time;
- using focused work periods;
- taking short breaks between longer work sessions;
- keeping notes about what I have completed and what I need to do next.

Things that distract me include frequent interruptions and having too many unclear tasks open at the same time.

The best way to support my productivity is to give me clear priorities and enough context to understand the expected outcome.

## Feedback & Growth

I prefer feedback to be clear, specific, and constructive.

It is especially helpful when feedback includes an example of what should be changed or improved.

If I make a mistake, I prefer someone to tell me directly and explain what needs to be corrected so I can learn from it.

For detailed feedback, I usually prefer written feedback because I can review it carefully while making changes.

I appreciate teammates who give feedback respectfully and recognise progress while also identifying areas that need improvement.

## What I Need From Teammates

To do my best work, I appreciate:

- clear instructions;
- realistic deadlines;
- direct communication;
- clear ownership of tasks;
- examples when requirements are complicated;
- enough context to understand why a task is being done;
- flexibility to ask questions when something is unclear.

## Things to Know About My Work Style

I like to understand the steps involved before making major changes.

For technical tasks, I often prefer to test one change at a time rather than changing many things at once.

I also like keeping evidence of completed work, such as test results, screenshots, Git commits, or documentation.

If I ask several questions about a task, it usually means I want to make sure I understand the requirements correctly before completing it.

## Fun Facts & Quirks

One thing people may misunderstand about me is that being quiet does not mean I am disengaged. I may simply be concentrating or processing information.

I enjoy learning new tools by actually using them rather than only reading about them.

My work superpower is persistence. If I run into a technical problem, I usually keep investigating and testing different solutions until I understand what went wrong.

## How This User Manual Can Help the Team

This user manual gives my teammates a clearer understanding of how I communicate, work, receive feedback, and stay productive.

Sharing these preferences can reduce misunderstandings and make collaboration easier because teammates know how to communicate with me and what helps me perform my best work.
