# Focus Bear Product Reflection

## What I learned

- Focus Bear combines focus sessions, routines, distraction blocking, schedules, and productivity habits.
- Users can customise focus and blocking settings, requiring reliable storage of user preferences.
- Scheduled features rely on timing and background processing.
- Backend data needs to remain consistent across the application.
- Security and privacy are important because the product handles user-related productivity settings.

## Reflection

Before reviewing the product, I mainly understood Focus Bear from a backend development perspective. Reviewing the product helped me understand how technical backend work supports the actual user experience.

Reliable APIs, background jobs, secure configuration, and consistent data handling are important because they directly affect how users experience focus sessions, schedules, and personalised settings.

I did not notice any obvious outdated or inconsistent help-centre content during my review.
