# Focus Bear First-Time Experience

## Overall Experience

I installed Focus Bear successfully and completed the onboarding process. The app was easy to install, but there were a few parts that were confusing as a first-time user.

## Friction Point 1

I was unsure what some focus modes meant during onboarding.

## Friction Point 2

There were many options on the screen at once, which felt overwhelming.

## Friction Point 3

Some settings were not clearly explained before asking me to choose them.

## Friction Point 4

I wasn't sure which features were free and which required payment.

## Friction Point 5

I wanted a short walkthrough after setup, but none appeared.

## Improvement Idea 1

Add a short guided tour after onboarding that explains the main features.

## Improvement Idea 2

Add simple descriptions under each setting so new users understand what each option does.

## Summary

Overall, I liked the app and think it has useful productivity features. With a clearer onboarding process, it would be easier for new users to get started.
